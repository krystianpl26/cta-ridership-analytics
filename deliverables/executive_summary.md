# Executive Summary
CTA ridership experienced a severe drop during the pandemic period and has been recovering unevenly across service types and day types. This analysis quantifies how total demand, bus/rail mix, and weekday-weekend behavior changed from 2001 through 2026, and benchmarks recovery versus the 2019 baseline.

## Business Questions
1. How has total CTA ridership changed year-over-year from 2001 to 2026?
2. Is ridership recovery stronger for bus or rail service?
3. How do weekday, Saturday, and Sunday/Holiday demand levels compare before and after the pandemic?
4. What monthly seasonality patterns can inform service planning?
5. How close is current ridership to 2019 demand levels?

## Key Findings
- Annual ridership shows a structural break beginning in 2020, followed by gradual multi-year recovery.
- Bus and rail recovered at different rates, changing the mode-share mix relative to pre-pandemic years.
- Weekday demand remains the largest volume driver, but weekend and holiday patterns provide useful signals for scheduling optimization.
- Monthly seasonality remains visible and can support resource planning for peak and shoulder periods.
- Recovery percentages vs 2019 provide a clear KPI for leadership reporting and service strategy checkpoints.

## Recommendations
- Use a rolling annual KPI dashboard (total rides, bus share, rail share, percent of 2019 baseline).
- Prioritize targeted service frequency improvements on corridors and lines with strongest rebound signals.
- Align seasonal operations plans to recurring monthly demand peaks.
- Reassess weekend service design using updated behavior patterns rather than relying on pre-2020 assumptions.

## Next Steps
- Add route-level and station-level analysis for more actionable service insights.
- Incorporate external drivers (weather, fuel prices, major events, hybrid work trends).
- Build an interactive dashboard (e.g., Tableau or Power BI) for stakeholder self-service.
- Implement forecasting once data quality checks and trend decomposition are complete.
